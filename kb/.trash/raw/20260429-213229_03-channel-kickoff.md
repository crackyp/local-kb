**Marcus Webb** — March 3 at 8:12 AM CT

Reading in. A few things from the Data Eng side before this goes too far:

1. The Snowflake → AEP nightly push is not broken. It does what it was designed to do. The constraint is that every new feature is a ticket, and the ticket queue is long because we're understaffed for MarTech demand.
2. "Bring transaction data into AEP" can mean a lot of things. It can mean "more features, faster." It can mean "raw events in AEP." Those are very different projects with very different costs and very different ownership models. Let's not conflate them in the vision deck.
3. Before we design anything, I want to understand what volume and what latency we're talking about. 11M transactions/day is not small. AEP pricing at that volume is meaningful.

Happy to do a working session once we have more clarity on the use cases. I don't want to architect in a vacuum.

**Will Hendricks** — March 3 at 9:20 AM CT

Marcus — understood and agreed. I'll treat the architecture question as open. My current mental model has three shapes (very rough):

**Shape 1 — Expanded feature push.** More features, same pattern. Cheapest, fastest. Still gated on Data Eng.

**Shape 2 — Trimmed transaction events in AEP.** Event-level data lands daily. Marketing authors features inside AEP. Much more flexible, higher ingestion cost.

**Shape 3 — Streaming.** Near-real-time events into AEP. Unlocks triggered journeys but is the biggest build.

I don't think Shape 3 is in scope for MVP. Between 1 and 2 is the real question. Will defer to you and Tom on what's feasible.

**Marcus Webb** — March 5 at 9:03 AM CT

Tom and I spent yesterday afternoon scoping Shape 2 at a rough level. A few early reactions:

- At 11M transactions/day, landing raw events in AEP is not a small ingestion bill. We'd want to trim the TSYS schema hard before ingestion — probably half the fields or less.
- Profile density will be a concern at a 13-month retention window. I don't have numbers yet but need to model it.
- Identity resolution is an open question — what do we use as the AEP profile linking key? Tokenized PAN is the obvious candidate but I want Infosec to confirm it's stable across card re-issuance before we commit.
- Merchant name normalization is a mess in the raw data. If we land raw strings, Marketing will get poor audiences. If we wait for the Merchant Taxonomy project, we wait indefinitely.

None of these are blockers. They're scoping items. I'll have a first architecture sketch for the working session on March 12.
