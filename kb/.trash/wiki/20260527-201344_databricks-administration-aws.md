# Databricks Administration on AWS

## Summary
This page outlines the core administration capabilities available in Databricks on AWS, providing a structured reference for managing accounts, workspaces, identities, and governance. It serves as a high-level index to official Databricks documentation, covering account settings, workspace deployment, identity management, compute policies, system tables, audit logs, and cost management tools.

### Account and Workspace Management
- **Administration Concepts:** Foundational information regarding administration privileges and responsibilities.
- **Account Administration:** Managing subscriptions, billing, serverless quotas, and account-level settings via the account console.
- **Workspace Deployment:** Creating and managing workspaces using serverless or traditional deployment methods.
- **Workspace Settings:** Configuring workspace-level features, settings, and Databricks Previews for individual environments.

### Identity and Access Management
- **Identity Management:** Managing users, groups, and service principals at both account and workspace levels using SCIM provisioning.
- **Compute Policies:** Establishing policies to control compute configuration, limit resource usage, and standardize compute environments.

### Monitoring and Governance
- **System Tables:** Accessing audit logs, billable usage data, lineage information, and operational data from the `system` catalog.
- **Audit Logs:** Reference of auditable events within the Databricks account.
- **Cost Management:** Monitoring and managing costs through usage dashboards, budget policies, and billing analysis tools.
