# Use Cases

Marketing use cases drive Flywheel scope decisions. The team identified MVP candidates through working sessions and stakeholder discussions.

## MVP Use Cases (Confirmed)

### UC1: Category-Based Offers

Trigger cardholders who spent over a threshold at restaurants (or other categories) in the last 30 days with targeted offers in the monthly offers journey. Currently blocked — requires a Data Eng ticket that takes 2–3 weeks and produces a one-time audience. With event-level data in AEP, Analytics can build this audience in Journey Optimizer without a Data Eng cycle.

**Business owner:** Greg Yamamoto
**Status:** MVP candidate

### UC2: Dormant Card Reactivation (90-Day Trigger)

Replace the current 180-day dormancy trigger with a 90-day trigger for existing winback journeys. Greg believes 90 days is better based on industry benchmarks — by 180 days, customers have already received three offers from Chase. Currently blocked because changing the threshold requires a new Data Eng ticket.

**Simplification from original ask:** Full quarter-over-quarter spend delta logic is Phase 2; "premium cardholder + no spend in 90 days" is MVP scope.

**Business owner:** Priya Raman / Lifecycle team
**Status:** MVP candidate

### UC3: Dispute Suppression

Suppress marketing outreach to customers with open fraud investigations. Currently reaching out with promotional offers to people in the middle of dispute resolution — a known customer experience problem. Binary signal (open dispute yes/no) lives in Fraud & Disputes system, not the card authorization feed, requiring a separate ingestion path.

Rachel Okonkwo noted this needs to be global (all marketing channels, not just card) to avoid rebuilding for Deposits in 6 months. 4-hour SLA from Fraud Ops needs confirmation.

**Business owner:** Rachel Okonkwo
**Status:** MVP candidate (pending SLA confirmation)

## Phase 2 Candidates

### Lapsed Premium Winback

Premium cardholders (Horizon, Reserve) whose spend dropped 40%+ quarter over quarter → winback offer. Requires complex logic (premium-tier identification + QoQ delta computation). Priya proposed simplified version for MVP: premium + 90-day dormancy.

### Travel Spend Identification

Target classic cardholders with high travel spend for the Horizon card relaunch. Time-boxed to Q3 relaunch (mid-September). If Flywheel misses the window, use case becomes moot.

### Cross-Sell (Card-to-Card)

Spend signals suggesting a classic cardholder is outgrowing the product → upgrade offer to Horizon. Inside Card org, so faster to scope than cross-org products. Requires definition work on what "outgrowing" means as a signal.

### New Cardholder Onboarding

Observe what a new cardholder does in their first 60 days and adjust the welcome journey accordingly. Flagged by Priya as a candidate worth considering.
